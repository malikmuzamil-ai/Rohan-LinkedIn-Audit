Critical Gaps
Top 3 Problems Killing Your Results
Problem 01
Your About section talks about you — not your reader

The About section opens with generic adjectives ("insightful, bold, creative, conscientious"). That's a self-description, not a hook. Visitors scanning your profile skip past any opening that isn't immediately about them.

What It's Costing You

Profile visitors click away in under 3 seconds. No one reaches out. The 14K followers you've built aren't converting to inbound conversations or opportunities because the profile doesn't speak to anyone's specific problem.

The Fix

Open with a line that names the reader's exact pain. "Most PMs know what good product management looks like in theory. Executing it inside a real company is a different problem." See the full rewrite in the About tab.

Problem 02
Your headline wastes 220 characters on a content statement

"I share what good product management looks like" is a content mission statement, not a value proposition. Worse, the employer disclaimer takes up 60+ characters of the most visible real estate on your profile. LinkedIn shows the headline everywhere — search results, comment sections, connection requests. It needs to do more.

What It's Costing You

ICP visitors can't immediately tell what they gain by following or connecting. The disclaimer signals corporate caution, not confident authority. Recruiters and founders who could hire you or partner with you move on to the next result.

The Fix

Lead with the specific outcome your audience gets. Move the disclaimer to the first line of your About section if it's legally required. See 3 headline options in the Headline tab — all under 150 characters.

Problem 03
Your niche is "product management" — which means you're for everyone

Product management is a broad category. The profile doesn't specify who you help most — aspiring PMs breaking in, mid-level PMs trying to get promoted, or startup founders building their first product team. Without that specificity, the profile resonates weakly with everyone.

What It's Costing You

You have 14K followers but no clear conversion mechanism. People follow for content but don't reach out because they don't know if you're the right person for their specific situation. Follower count without inbound DMs means the content engine is running but the sales engine isn't.

The Fix

Pick your primary ICP and name them explicitly across every section. The rewrites in this report are built around aspiring and mid-level PMs who want real-world frameworks from someone inside a major tech company. Adjust if your actual ICP differs.
